Get the real open booking times at this business. Call this whenever the contact asks about availability, for example "do you have anything tomorrow?", "can I come Friday?", "what's open next week?" or "anything after 3?". Never guess or invent times, and never calculate availability yourself; this tool is the only source of truth.

Input:
- date: the day to check, as YYYY-MM-DD in the business's local timezone. Work out relative words like "tomorrow" or "Friday" from today's date.

Result:
- configured=false means the business hasn't set up its schedule. Don't offer any times; tell the contact a team member will confirm a time, and hand off if appropriate.
- slots is the list of open times. Each slot has start and end with the business's timezone offset. Present them naturally in local time, for example "2:00 PM, 3:00 PM or 4:15 PM". Don't show the raw timestamp or the offset.
- If slots is empty, say nothing is available that day and offer to check another day. Don't invent alternatives.
- If the contact wants a time window like "afternoon" or "after 3", filter the returned slots yourself. Don't offer times that weren't returned.
- existingUpcomingBookings and canCreateNewBooking describe the contact's CURRENT bookings, separate from the requested date.
The result includes today, tomorrow and their weekday names in the business's timezone. Always use those to resolve words like "tomorrow", "Friday" or "next week"; never assume the current date yourself.

Only offer times from this tool. Once the contact picks one, return action=schedule.
